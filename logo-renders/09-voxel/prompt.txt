A polished brand presentation of a minimalist pixel-art logo on a flat near-black background (#14110e). The mark sits on an invisible 16x16 square pixel grid: a solid 3x3-pixel amber (#f0a85a) square at the lower left and an identical 3x3 square at the upper right, joined by a thin one-pixel-high stepped diagonal line made of four 2x1 pixel segments, each one step up and to the right, touching only at their corners, in the 2:1 slope of an isometric pixel-art cube edge. The result is a clean "vertex, edge, vertex" bitmap. It has hard square pixel edges and no anti-aliasing, gradients, glow, shadows or texture: flat vector, with generous negative space. To the right, the words "Edge Solutions" are set in a custom 5x7 square pixel font in warm off-white (#ede6d8), with the pixel size half that of the mark's pixels, and the baseline aligned with the bottom of the lower-left square. There is no other text. The overall feel is crisp, technical and calm, like a premium terminal UI.
