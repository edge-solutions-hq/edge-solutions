A polished flat-vector brand presentation of a monogram logo on a solid #14110e near-black background. The mark is the letters "E" and "S" built from thick, blocky, equal-weight square strokes on a 2:1 isometric grid: all horizontal strokes rise to the right at about 26.6 degrees (2 across, 1 up), all vertical strokes stay perfectly vertical, so the letters look printed on the right face of an isometric cube. The E (spine plus top and bottom arms) sits on the left, a one-stroke gap, then a squared S (top bar, upper-left vertical, lower-right vertical, bottom bar). One long slanted bar crosses the full width at mid-height, acting as both the E's middle arm and the S's middle stroke, extending half a stroke past the E's spine on the left and past the S on the right; it is colored light amber #ffc686 while the rest of the letters are amber #f0a85a, and thin dark cuts separate the bar from the vertical strokes it touches. Crisp hard edges, no gradients, no glow, no bevel, no extra shapes, generous empty space, centered. Optional second panel: the same mark left of the words "Edge Solutions" set in a light cream monospace font (JetBrains Mono style), "Edge" bold and "Solutions" regular.
